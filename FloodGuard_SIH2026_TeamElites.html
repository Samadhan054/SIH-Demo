Problem Statement SIH26192
❌ THE PROBLEM
  • Flash floods in Himalayan basins strike within minutes of cloudburst — zero lead time
  • Traditional downstream gauge systems miss upstream gorge funneling dynamics
  • No unified multi-source platform (rain + river + soil + DEM)
  • 4,000+ lives lost annually to floods in India (NDMA 2023)
  • Cellular blackout prevents SOS signals during peak disaster
  • Rescue dispatch is manual, slow, and lacks priority scoring
✅ OUR SOLUTION: FloodGuard
  • Multi-source risk engine: Rainfall + River Rise + CWC Thresholds + Soil Saturation + DEM slope
  • 28+ real Indian rivers registered with CWC gauge threshold bands
  • Automatic danger breach detection → instant SMS alerts + rescue priority queue
  • Satellite SOS: 128-byte compressed binary packet — works with ZERO cellular
  • GIS interactive risk map with real-time rescue lifecycle tracking
  • RBAC portal for Citizens, Rescue Teams, Govt & Admins

📐 RISK ENGINE FORMULA
RiskScore =
  0.30 × F_rain
+ 0.25 × F_rise
+ 0.15 × F_level
+ 0.15 × F_threshold (CWC)
+ 0.08 × F_soil (NASA SMAP)
+ 0.07 × F_dem (Slope/Valley)
Output bands: LOW · MODERATE · HIGH · SEVERE (0.0 – 1.0)

⭐ 8 KEY DIFFERENTIATORS THAT MAKE FLOODGUARD STAND OUT ⭐
🛰️
SATELLITE SOS — ZERO NETWORK

128-byte compressed binary packet SOS protocol using Iridium SBD/Skylo modem — the ONLY solution that works when cellular towers fail during peak disaster. Critical for mountain victims.

🌊
CHANNEL-SHAPE CWC THRESHOLDS

Different thresholds for gorge/confined, braided, meandering, and deltaic river types. No other open-source system adapts warning levels to channel morphology.

🧠
SIX-PARAMETER WEIGHTED RISK ENGINE

Combines Rainfall + River Rise Rate + Water Level + CWC Breach + Soil Moisture (NASA SMAP) + DEM Valley Slope into a single normalized score — not just one or two sensors.

🗺️
ACTIONABLE RESCUE MAP

Rescue Teams can advance SOS status directly from map popups in real time. Dispatchers no longer need to switch between separate systems — saves critical minutes.

WORKING PROTOTYPE READY NOW
